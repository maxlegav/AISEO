# Product Brief: ShowYourBrand

## Executive Summary

**ShowYourBrand** is the first accessible SEO + GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) audit platform designed for the AI search era. As user behavior shifts from Google to AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek), businesses face a critical new risk: **complete invisibility**. Unlike traditional search where a business eventually appears by scrolling, AI engines can entirely omit businesses from their responses—making GEO optimization increasingly urgent.

ShowYourBrand bridges the gap between **non-technical business owners** and **developers** through a dual-level approach: a beautiful, simple dashboard for instant visibility assessment, and comprehensive technical reports that guide precise implementation. Distributed primarily through **white-label partnerships with marketing agencies** and adopted by **enterprise clients**, ShowYourBrand positions businesses to thrive in the emerging AI search landscape.

**The Urgency**: As AI-powered search grows exponentially, GEO visibility isn't optional—it's existential. ShowYourBrand is the first mover defining this new category before giants realize the opportunity.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Businesses are facing an accelerating crisis: **invisibility in AI search**.

When potential customers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or DeepSeek for recommendations, many businesses are **completely ignored**—not ranked poorly, but never mentioned at all. This represents a fundamentally different and more severe problem than poor Google rankings:

- **Google**: Scroll to page 10, you'll eventually find the business
- **AI Assistants**: The business may **never appear**, regardless of how relevant they are

**The Behavioral Shift**: Search behavior is rapidly transitioning from traditional search engines to AI assistants. Every month, more users query AI directly instead of Google. This shift makes GEO visibility not just important, but **increasingly critical to business survival**.

### Problem Impact

**For Businesses:**

- Lost revenue from customers who never discover them via AI search
- Growing competitive disadvantage as AI-savvy competitors gain visibility
- No metrics or visibility into how AI engines perceive their business
- Risk compounds daily as more searches migrate to AI platforms

**For Marketing Agencies:**

- Clients asking about AI visibility with no tools to provide answers
- Missed opportunity to offer a valuable new service
- Unable to measure ROI on content optimization for AI crawlers

**The Escalating Crisis**: Unlike SEO (a mature, stable market), GEO is **rapidly becoming more important**. Every passing month increases the percentage of searches conducted via AI, making this problem more acute and urgent.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

**Traditional SEO Tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz):**

- ❌ **No GEO Focus**: Designed for Google, not AI assistants
- ❌ **Complexity Barrier**: Built for SEO experts, intimidating for business owners
- ❌ **Jargon-Heavy**: Technical language inaccessible to non-developers
- ❌ **12,000 Features**: Overwhelming dashboards that obscure key insights

**Current Approach Gap**:

- No tool bridges the communication gap between business owners and developers
- Business owners can't assess their AI visibility
- Developers receive vague requests: "make us show up in ChatGPT"
- No actionable, specific guidance exists

**Market Timing**: The GEO market is **undefined**. No standards exist. No dominant players have emerged. This is a **first-mover opportunity** before the giants (Ahrefs, SEMrush) pivot to address AI search.

### Proposed Solution

**ShowYourBrand** is the first **simple, beautiful audit platform** that reveals business visibility in AI search engines and provides actionable optimization guidance.

**Dual-Level Architecture**:

1. **Simple Dashboard** (Business Owner Interface):
   - Visual, beautiful, **glanceable** interface—understand in 5 seconds
   - 3-level priority system (🔴 Critical / 🟠 Important / 🟢 Nice-to-have)
   - "GEO Health Score" with clear, plain-language recommendations
   - **Zero intimidation**: Designed to NOT scare non-technical users

2. **Technical Report** (Developer Implementation):
   - Downloadable comprehensive PDF with precise technical guidance
   - Specific HTML tag modifications: "Add this schema markup on line 42"
   - Code snippets ready to copy-paste
   - Detailed technical rationale for developers

**Core Features (MVP)**:

- HTML Health Scanner with 3-level prioritization
- Visual Site Health Dashboard with GEO score
- Prompt Gap Analysis (compare visibility vs competitors in AI responses)
- Alt Text Opportunity Finder (AI-generated recommendations)
- FAQ Generator from common AI prompts
- One-Click Schema Injector with implementation guidance
- Weekly GEO Health Email (gamified, 1 action/week)

**Architecture**:

- **Next.js Frontend/Backend** (Vercel): Dashboard, auth, payments, database
- **Docker Scraping Service**: Heavy processing (AI API calls, web scraping, analysis)
- **Two-Entity Separation**: Clean, scalable, optimized for each responsibility

### Key Differentiators

**1. GEO Pioneer Advantage**

- **First accessible GEO tool** before the market is defined
- Opportunity to **create the standard** (like Domain Authority for SEO)
- 12-18 month window before giants respond

**2. Radical Simplicity for Non-Technical Users**

- **Anti-Ahrefs**: Accessible, not intimidating
- Beautiful, visual dashboard that business owners **enjoy** using
- Communication bridge: Empowers business owners to guide developers

**3. Dual-Level Sophistication**

- **Simple for decision-makers**, technical for implementers
- Dashboard shows "what" and "why" (business value)
- Report shows "how" and "where" (implementation specifics)

**4. White-Label Distribution Strategy**

- **Marketing agencies** as primary go-to-market channel
- Agencies offer ShowYourBrand audits as premium service to clients
- Scalable distribution without massive sales team
- Win-win: Agencies expand offerings, ShowYourBrand scales rapidly

**5. Killer Marketing Message**

- **"L'IA peut ne JAMAIS parler de vous"** (AI may never mention you)
- Taps into legitimate FOMO—worse than Google page 10
- Positions GEO as **urgent business risk**, not nice-to-have

**6. Premium Positioning**

- **Quality signaling through pricing** (not race to bottom)
- Clear tier structure: Basic €100 (one-shot) / Pro €200 (one-shot) / Premium €500/mo (subscription)
- One-shots for individual audits, subscription for agencies with volume
- Attracts serious clients (enterprises + agencies), not tire-kickers

**7. MVP-First Pragmatism**

- Ruthless prioritization—ship fast, learn fast
- No feature bloat, no over-engineering
- Focus on **lançable** (launchable) product, iterate based on feedback

## MVP Scope

### Core Features (MVP - Phase 1)

The MVP focuses on **7 core features** that deliver immediate value to agencies and enterprises while remaining ruthlessly focused on "lançable" (launchable) product principles.

#### 1. 🔴 HTML Health Scanner

**What:** Automated scan of website HTML for GEO optimization opportunities
**Why Critical:** Foundation of entire audit - identifies technical gaps in AI crawlability
**Deliverable:**

- Scan all pages for schema markup presence/absence
- Check meta descriptions, title tags, H1-H6 structure
- Validate structured data (JSON-LD) implementation
- **3-Level Priority System**: 🔴 Critical / 🟠 Important / 🟢 Nice-to-have

**MVP Constraints:**

- Limit to 50 pages per audit (prevents overload, keeps costs manageable)
- Focus on schema.org Person, Organization, FAQPage, Product schemas only

---

#### 2. 🔴 Visual Site Health Dashboard

**What:** Beautiful, simple dashboard showing GEO health score and top priorities
**Why Critical:** Non-technical users need glanceable visibility - the "5-second understanding"
**Deliverable:**

- **GEO Health Score** (0-100%) with color-coded visual (red/yellow/green)
- Top 5 issues displayed prominently with plain-language descriptions
- Clear next actions: "Add FAQ schema to your About page"
- Progress tracking over time (if monthly subscription)

**MVP Constraints:**

- Dashboard shows aggregated score only (no drill-down into every detail)
- Focus on clarity over comprehensiveness

---

#### 3. 🟠 Prompt Gap Analysis

**What:** Test 100-500 AI prompts to see if business appears in responses
**Why Important:** Direct measurement of AI visibility - shows exactly where business is invisible
**Deliverable:**

- Run predefined battery of prompts against ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity
- Report: "You appear in 23/100 prompts (23% visibility)"
- Show **competitor comparison**: "Competitor X appears in 67/100 prompts"
- Highlight prompt categories where visibility is lowest

**MVP Constraints:**

- Basic tier: 100 prompts, Pro tier: 250 prompts, Premium tier: 500 prompts
- Prompts are pre-defined templates (not custom per business yet)
- 3-5 competitor analysis only

---

#### 4. 🟢 Alt Text Opportunity Finder

**What:** AI-powered analysis of images missing alt text with auto-generated suggestions
**Why Nice-to-Have:** Quick win for users - easy implementation with high GEO impact
**Deliverable:**

- Scan all images on site for missing/poor alt text
- Use AI (GPT-4V or Claude Vision) to analyze image content
- Suggest descriptive, GEO-optimized alt text: "Professional chef preparing organic pasta dish in modern kitchen"

**MVP Constraints:**

- Limit to 50 images per audit
- AI suggestions are guidance only (not auto-implemented)

---

#### 5. 🟢 FAQ Generator

**What:** Generate FAQ schema markup from common AI prompts about the business
**Why Nice-to-Have:** FAQPage schema directly influences AI responses - high-value, easy win
**Deliverable:**

- Analyze top prompts related to business category
- Generate 10-15 FAQ questions + answers
- Provide ready-to-implement JSON-LD schema code

**Example:**

- Prompt: "What restaurants in Paris serve gluten-free options?"
- FAQ: "Does [Business Name] offer gluten-free menu items?"
- Answer: [AI-generated based on site content]

**MVP Constraints:**

- Generic FAQs based on industry templates (not deeply customized)
- 10-15 FAQs maximum per audit

---

#### 6. 🔴 One-Click Schema Injector

**What:** Visual guide for implementing schema markup with copy-paste code snippets
**Why Critical:** Bridge gap between audit insights and implementation - removes friction
**Deliverable:**

- For each identified schema opportunity, provide:
  - **Exact code snippet** (JSON-LD format)
  - **Where to add it**: "Add to <head> section of homepage"
  - **Visual guide**: Screenshot or diagram showing implementation location
- Copy-paste ready - no technical expertise required

**MVP Constraints:**

- Focus on JSON-LD format only (not Microdata or RDFa)
- Cover 5 core schema types: Organization, Person, Product, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList

---

#### 7. 🟠 Weekly GEO Health Email

**What:** Gamified weekly email with 1 actionable task to improve GEO score
**Why Important:** Drives engagement, keeps GEO top-of-mind, encourages implementation
**Deliverable:**

- Weekly email (Sunday evening) with:
  - Current GEO score vs last week
  - **One specific action**: "This week: Add FAQ schema to your Services page (estimated impact: +5 points)"
  - Motivational copy: "You're in the top 30% of ShowYourBrand users!"
  - Link to dashboard for full report

**MVP Constraints:**

- Only for monthly subscription users (not one-time audits)
- Pre-written action templates (not deeply personalized)
