Before investing in any optimization strategy, you need to know where you stand today. This 10-point self-audit will help you identify the biggest gaps in your current AI visibility, and understand what a professional GEO audit can reveal that this self-assessment can't.

Work through each point honestly. Score yourself **0** (not done), **1** (partially done), or **2** (fully done). Then see the scoring guide at the end.

---

## Point 1: AI Crawler Access (0 or 2)

Go to `yourdomain.com/robots.txt`. Are GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot listed with `Allow: /`?

**Score 0:** Any of these bots are blocked or your robots.txt doesn't explicitly allow them. You may be invisible to one or more major AI platforms.

**Score 2:** All three are explicitly allowed.

If you're on Cloudflare, also verify that Bot Fight Mode or AI bot blocking is not enabled at the CDN level.

---

## Point 2: llms.txt Presence (0 or 2)

Go to `yourdomain.com/llms.txt`. Does the file exist and contain a structured description of your company?

**Score 0:** File doesn't exist.

**Score 2:** File exists with accurate company description, key pages, and official profile links.

---

## Point 3: Organization Schema (0, 1, or 2)

Use Google's Rich Results Test or a schema validator to check whether your homepage has Organization Schema markup.

**Score 0:** No schema.

**Score 1:** Schema exists but is missing the `sameAs` property linking to your social/directory profiles.

**Score 2:** Schema exists and includes name, URL, logo, description, and multiple `sameAs` links.

---

## Point 4: FAQ Schema on Key Pages (0, 1, or 2)

Do your key content pages (especially blog posts and product pages) include FAQ sections with FAQ schema markup?

**Score 0:** No FAQ sections.

**Score 1:** FAQ sections exist but no schema markup.

**Score 2:** FAQ sections with validated FAQ schema markup.

---

## Point 5: Content Opening Quality (0, 1, or 2)

Open your three most important pages. Does the first paragraph answer the main question directly, with specific facts?

**Score 0:** Introductory preamble, no direct answer.

**Score 1:** Answer is present but buried.

**Score 2:** First sentence answers the main question directly, with a specific, citable fact or definition.

---

## Point 6: External Citation Footprint (0, 1, or 2)

Search your brand name on Google. Count the number of third-party, independent websites that mention your brand on the first page of results.

**Score 0:** Only your own website and social profiles appear.

**Score 1:** 1-3 independent mentions (reviews, directories, one or two articles).

**Score 2:** 5+ independent mentions from diverse, authoritative sources.

---

## Point 7: Reddit Presence (0, 1, or 2)

Search `site:reddit.com "[your brand name]"` in Google. Are there positive discussions about your brand in relevant subreddits?

**Score 0:** No Reddit mentions, or only negative ones.

**Score 1:** A few mentions, mostly neutral.

**Score 2:** Multiple positive, substantive discussions referencing your brand in relevant subreddits.

---

## Point 8: Content Freshness (0, 1, or 2)

When were your key pages last updated? And does your sitemap's `lastmod` tag reflect those actual update dates?

**Score 0:** Core pages haven't been updated in 12+ months, or `lastmod` is set to the sitemap generation date.

**Score 1:** Updated within the past year, but not regularly.

**Score 2:** Core pages updated within the past 3 to 6 months with accurate `lastmod` tags.

---

## Point 9: Manual AI Mention Test (0, 1, or 2)

Open ChatGPT and ask: "What's the best [your category] for [your target use case]?" and "What are your thoughts on [your brand]?"

**Score 0:** Not mentioned, or mentioned negatively.

**Score 1:** Mentioned once, neutrally, without specific positive attributes.

**Score 2:** Mentioned with positive framing and specific attributes accurately described.

---

## Point 10: Competitor Benchmarking (0, 1, or 2)

Ask ChatGPT the same queries you tested in Point 9, and note how your top two competitors are described by comparison.

**Score 0:** Competitors mentioned prominently, you are not.

**Score 1:** Both you and competitors mentioned but competitors more favorably.

**Score 2:** You are mentioned at least as favorably as your main competitors.
