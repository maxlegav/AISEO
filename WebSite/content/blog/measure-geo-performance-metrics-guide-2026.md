"You can't improve what you don't measure." This cliché has never been more relevant than in GEO. Because unlike traditional SEO, where Google Search Console gives you a fairly clear picture of rankings and clicks. AI visibility is largely invisible without deliberate measurement.

Your analytics platform doesn't tell you how often ChatGPT mentions your brand. Your rank tracker doesn't track your position in Perplexity responses. Without active measurement, you're flying blind.

This post covers the metrics that matter and the tools and approaches to track them.

## The 6 Core GEO Metrics

**1. AI Mention Rate**
The most fundamental metric: out of 100 prompts relevant to your category and brand, how many include your brand in the response? This is your AI visibility percentage. Test a defined set of prompts across multiple AI platforms monthly and track the trend.

**2. Sentiment Score**
Being mentioned is necessary but not sufficient. How you're mentioned matters enormously. Define a simple scoring system: +1 for positive mentions (recommended, described as a leader, positive attributes cited), 0 for neutral mentions (listed as an option without positive framing), -1 for negative mentions (problems cited, comparative disadvantages highlighted). Track your average sentiment score monthly.

**3. Competitor Share of AI Mentions**
Look at the same 100 prompts and track how often your top 3 competitors are mentioned. Your relative share tells you whether you're winning, losing, or holding ground. If your mention rate is 40% and your main competitor's is 70%, you have a quantified gap to close.

**4. Cross-Platform Consistency**
AI platforms have different biases and draw from different sources. Track your mention rate separately for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Brands with strong GEO appear consistently across all platforms. Brands with weak GEO often dominate on one platform and are absent from others.

**5. Source Citation Tracking**
When AI mentions your brand, what sources does it cite? Track which third-party sources are being used to substantiate mentions of your brand. This tells you which external content is actually driving your AI visibility, and where you should focus your external citation building.

**6. AI Referral Traffic and Conversion**
In Google Analytics 4 or equivalent, segment traffic by referrer to identify sessions coming from AI platforms. These sessions will typically show referrers from chat.openai.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai, etc. Track conversion rates for these segments separately, this is where you'll validate the 2.3x conversion rate advantage.

## Manual vs Automated Tracking

**Manual approach** (free, time-intensive): Create a spreadsheet with 20 to 50 key prompts. Each month, test each prompt across your target AI platforms. Record the response, presence, sentiment, and sources cited. This is feasible for small brands or as a starting point.

**Automated approach** (paid, scalable): Platforms like Promptmonitor, SE Ranking's AI Visibility Tracker, and others run your defined prompts automatically across multiple AI platforms and return structured data on visibility, sentiment, and sources. The cost is typically $30 to $100+ per month, but the time savings are substantial when you're tracking 50+ prompts across 4+ platforms.

## What Your Measurement Cadence Should Look Like

**Weekly:** Monitor for any sharp changes in sentiment or mention rate, particularly if you've made significant changes to your content, received notable press coverage, or are aware of a competitor campaign.

**Monthly:** Run a full prompt set across all target platforms. Record mention rates, sentiment scores, competitor share, and source citations. Compare to prior month.

**Quarterly:** Deep-dive analysis. Update your prompt set to reflect new queries your customers are actually asking. Audit your technical configuration (robots.txt, llms.txt, schema). Review which content changes from the previous quarter correlated with visibility improvements.
