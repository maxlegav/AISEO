This is the story of how a mid-sized B2B SaaS brand — a project management tool targeting SMBs — went from being completely absent from AI responses to being regularly recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude within a 90-day window.

The tactics are replicable. The timeline is realistic. And the results should make clear why GEO investment pays off faster than most marketing channels.

## The Starting Point: Invisible

At the beginning of the engagement, we ran a baseline audit across 100 prompts on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. The prompts ranged from category-level queries ("what's the best project management tool for a 15-person team?") to direct brand queries ("what do people think of [brand]?") to competitive comparisons.

The results: **mentioned in 4 out of 100 prompts**. In two of those four mentions, the sentiment was neutral to negative. In the other two, the mention was brief and non-differentiating.

Their competitors were mentioned in 40-70% of relevant prompts, with positive, specific descriptions.

## Month 1: Fix the Foundation

**Week 1** was entirely technical. robots.txt was blocking GPTBot and PerplexityBot — an old setting from a Cloudflare security configuration that nobody had reviewed. Fixed in 20 minutes. llms.txt was created from scratch.

**Weeks 2-3** focused on content structure. The top 10 most important pages on the site — homepage, product pages, key feature pages — were restructured with:
- Clear opening paragraphs answering the core question
- Quick Answer boxes added at the top
- FAQ sections appended to each page
- FAQ schema markup implemented

**Week 4** was schema markup. Organization Schema, SoftwareApplication Schema, and FAQ Schema were implemented and validated using Google's Rich Results Test.

## Month 2: Build External Presence

The technical work unlocks the door. The external work builds the authority.

**Reddit:** A founder account was created and spent 3 weeks building karma in r/projectmanagement, r/SaaS, and r/startups — answering questions, sharing genuine insights, contributing to ongoing discussions. No promotional content at all in this phase.

**Guest posts:** Two guest posts were placed on industry publications (DR 45+), each mentioning the brand in context as an example solution. Both were published by day 45.

**PR:** Three HARO responses were submitted to journalist queries about productivity tools. One was published in a recognized industry blog by day 52.

**Existing high-ranking content:** Three competitor comparison articles that were already being cited by AI were identified. Outreach was sent to two of them. The brand was added to a "best project management tools" article with DR 58 within 10 days.

## Month 3: Compound and Monitor

By day 60, AI mentions had increased from **4% to 31%**. By day 75, the number was 47%. By day 90, the brand was being mentioned in **62% of relevant prompts**, with consistently positive sentiment.

The key shift wasn't a single action. It was the combination: technical access + structured content + external citations + community presence. Each layer fed the others.

## The Business Impact

Traffic from AI platforms increased by roughly 40% over 90 days in absolute terms. More importantly, the conversion rate from AI-referred visitors was 2.1x higher than from organic Google traffic. The compound effect on pipeline was significant.
