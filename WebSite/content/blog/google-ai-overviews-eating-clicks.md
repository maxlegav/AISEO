Here's an uncomfortable number: Google AI Overviews now appear on at least 13% of all search results pages. For many high-value informational queries in categories like software, finance, health, and education, that number is significantly higher.

What does an AI Overview do? It answers the user's question directly at the top of the page, before any organic results, before any paid ads. It gives them what they came for without requiring them to click anything.

The result for brands that ranked well in traditional organic search: **click-through rates dropping. Traffic declining. Rankings unchanged — but meaningless.**

If you haven't already noticed this in your Google Search Console data, check your click-through rates on informational queries from the past 12 months. The pattern is likely visible.

## How Google AI Overviews Decide What to Include

Google's AI Overviews are powered by a large language model that synthesizes content from across the web. But because Google owns the search experience, it integrates AI Overviews directly into the SERP — and it controls which sources it pulls from.

Research from Search Engine Land found that **50% of sources cited in AI Overviews also rank in positions 4 through 10** in the regular organic results. This means you don't have to rank number one to appear in AI Overviews — but you probably do need to rank on the first page.

Additionally, YouTube is heavily featured in Google's AI Overviews. Analysis from Moz shows that YouTube videos appear disproportionately often in AI Overview responses, especially for how-to and tutorial queries.

## The Three Ways to Appear in Google AI Overviews

**1. Rank in the top 10 organic results.**
This is the foundational requirement. AI Overviews draw heavily from established, ranking content. If you're not ranking, you're unlikely to be cited in AI Overviews.

**2. Create video content on YouTube.**
For any query where a video tutorial would be relevant, YouTube is your fastest path to AI Overview inclusion. A screen recording with clear narration, uploaded with a keyword-rich title, detailed description, and transcript, is enough to compete in many categories.

**3. Implement strong structured data.**
FAQ schema, in particular, is heavily leveraged by Google's AI Overviews. Pages with explicit Q&A markup in schema have a significantly higher rate of AI Overview inclusion for the questions they answer.

## What's Different About AI Overviews vs Other AI Platforms

Unlike ChatGPT or Perplexity, Google AI Overviews appear *in the search experience* — users don't have to opt into a different product. This means the audience is enormous. Billions of searches monthly, across every demographic.

But it also means the optimization levers are somewhat different. Google AI Overviews strongly favor content that Google's own ranking signals validate. It's the most SEO-dependent of all the AI search surfaces. Your traditional SEO investment pays double dividends here: ranking organically AND being cited in the AI Overview for the same query.

## The Integrated Strategy

The optimal approach for 2026 is not to optimize for AI Overviews *or* for ChatGPT *or* for Perplexity as separate efforts. The tactics overlap significantly: strong SEO foundation, structured content, schema markup, external citations, YouTube presence. Build those correctly and you benefit across all AI surfaces simultaneously.

The question is knowing which AI surfaces you're currently performing well on, which you're absent from, and which represent the biggest opportunity for your specific category.
